  Direction inverted: the sampler now hunts the DARKEST point under each token.
  No logic change was needed — it minimises contrast RATIO, so it follows the
  token rather than assuming a direction. A test pins that both ways.

  Measured over an AREA (blur sigma 4), not a pixel. The tokens are opaque, so
  nothing shows through a digit and the token is judged on its silhouette; one
  dark speck hides nothing, a dark patch hides an edge.

  YOUR 0.3537 FIGURE is a darkest-AREA measure, not a darkest-pixel one. Per-pixel
  minimum for world 3 is 0.1424. Blur sigma 8 gives 0.3564, and world 3 is the
  darkest of the set by that measure — matching exactly. The gate uses sigma 4,
  deliberately tighter than the statistic behind the approved number.

  MONOTONIC ORDERING: assertion deleted, not skipped.

STRETCH — REPLACED, not re-run. Uniform scale + centre crop.
  You expected the trapezoid to behave better on flat textures. It measures worse,
  for a reason the old metric could not see: on a ruled surface the content IS the
  regularity, so a non-uniform stretch is a bulge.
  Grid period per decile, world 1:  stretch 52-69px (CoV 9.0%) -> cover 54-56px (CoV 1.1%)
  CoV, stretch vs cover:  w1 9.0%->1.1%   w2 20.7%->0.0%   w4 37.3%->0.9%
  World 2's stretch smeared the ruling past detection entirely in deciles 2-7.
  These are surfaces, not compositions — no subject to protect — so 36% of the
  width can simply be cropped and nothing is distorted at all. That option did not
  exist for the landscapes, where the reeds and rock columns lived at the edges.

BACKDROP — re-derived from scratch, 20% WHITE (was 50-60% dark)
  Colour first: white. The tokens are the dark things now, so a dark veil would
  pull the ground toward them AND mute the paper the direction rests on. White
  pushes the ground away, so separation and contrast stop competing.
  Alpha from measurement: a band edge reads as an edge at ~3x the local grain.
  Grain sd 4.02-4.78 across the set -> alpha 0.175-0.204. Shipped 0.20 for the
  grainiest surface; 80% of the texture survives. The gate still measures the
  BARE background, so this is free margin.

WORLD 2 — ruled lines vs tile rows: NO interference.
  Ruling period 31.7 design px (68px in the 900x2100 source, cover-fit at 0.4667).
  Tile row pitch 54, target row pitch 52. Ratio 1.70 — incommensurate, so lines
  never align systematically with row edges and there is no moiré. Lines pass
  BEHIND opaque tiles and never touch a digit; the tiles' own drop shadows are far
  darker than the ruling. See 06 for the close-up.
