Lane Math — Phase 4A review: persistence and economy
Stars, lives, save data. Hints/shop/ads/gold are 4B and absent.
Mode is Casual (§6 = unlimited operators), so operator counts are gone from the board.

Also fixes a Phase 3 report: "front target 45 with no way to reach it and the game
didn't fail". The game DID fail — failure detection was correct and is now covered by
a replay test across five levels. The failure was invisible: FAILED lived in a status
strip at y=800 on a fixed 900px canvas, and a shorter window cut it off below the pool.
Two fixes: the canvas now scales to the viewport, and the terminal state draws a banner
across the lane where the player is actually looking.
