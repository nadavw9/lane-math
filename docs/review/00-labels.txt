                 Green sits opposite both blue and sand on the hue circle, so it

STRETCH — DEVIATED FROM THE 40% BAND, DELIBERATELY
  All four sources are 1024x1536 and need +853px to reach 21:9.
  The specified central-40% band concentrates all of it into 614 rows: a 2.39x
  local scale, with vertical detail in the band retained at only 30-35% of
  original and a centre/outer detail ratio of 0.57-0.71 — the centre visibly
  softer than its surroundings. Worlds 2 and 3 carry MORE detail in the centre
  than at the edges (2.32 vs 2.07, and 2.01 vs 1.46), which is the reeds and
  rock columns.
  A raised-cosine taper over 70% was WORSE at the peak (2.59x) — it piles every
  extra pixel at the exact centre.
  Shipped: 80% band, trapezoid profile with 25% ramps. Peak 1.93x, retention
  36-43%, centre/outer 0.77-1.02. World 3, the worst case, lands at 1.02 —
  the stretched region now reads at the same detail density as the untouched
  margins. Scale is continuous at the seams, so there is no crease.
  Re-run any single world with:  node tools/process-backgrounds.mjs 3
