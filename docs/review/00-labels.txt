p5-01-tokens.png
    Level 4-01. Tokens are PROCEDURAL (§9.2 as amended) — Graphics + text, no
    atlas, no compression step, no resolution ceiling.
    SHAPE-CODED, not colour-coded:
      hexagonal plates   targets, cool, FLAT and recessed — you spend tiles ON
                         them, so they must not read as pickable
      rounded squares    pool numbers, warm, bevelled, drop shadow — Scrabble
                         weight, a finite thing you spend
      circles            operators, unmistakably not numbers
    Empty equation slots are dashed sockets, so the row reads as a sentence with
    gaps. Dimmed tiles lose their bevel as well as their colour, so bold-active/
    dim-inactive (§3.5) is carried by FORM as well as hue.
    Note the target plate colour changed this phase — see the gate note below.

p5-02-shatter.png
    Mid-commit, slowed ~16x to be photographable. 9 + 5 = 14 has just been
    committed: the operator has burst into green wedges in its slot and the two
    number tiles are breaking apart in the pool, all of it thrown INTO the

    "gone forever" legible. They are stroke-only at 22% so they do not compete
    with live tiles.

p5-03-failure-pulse.png
    The failure moment, rebuilt per §9.4. The front target 2 has gone red and
    been SHOVED out of alignment with the plates below it — the lane rejecting

p5-04-brightness-gate.txt
    The CI gate, green on placeholders. Four flat vignetted gradients at
    720x1560, sampled with the Traffic Bomb 5-point median under the lane,
    operator and pool zones, against the REAL token colours, at three aspects
    including the extremes (§13) rather than only the 9:21 design ratio.

    The gate earned itself immediately: it failed at 1.6:1, and the cause was
    the TOKEN, not the image. targetPlate was 0x2c3a52, dark enough that 3:1
    against it is mathematically unreachable — even a pure black background
    reaches only 1.84:1, so the plate read as a hole rather than an object.
    Lightened to 0x5a6a92 and the gate clears at 3.10-3.21:1 everywhere.
    That is the gate doing its job before any real art exists.

    Real backgrounds drop into assets/bg/ as world-N.webp. The gate REFUSES to
    skip a format it cannot decode — a silently skipped image is how Traffic
    Bomb shipped placeholder bugs.
